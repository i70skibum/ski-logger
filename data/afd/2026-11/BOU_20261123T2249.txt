.DISCUSSION /Through Monday/...
Issued at 1033 PM MST Mon Nov 23 2026

Isolated light snow showers will continue overnight for the higher 
terrain, with precip chances ending by early tomorrow morning as 
the upper level shortwave and jet continue moving off to the 
east. We're already seeing some patchy fog develop along the CO/WY
border late this evening. Patchy fog could spread further south 
and west overnight, potentially impacting the northern I-25 
corridor. But fog is expected to stay north of the metro.

Quiet weather is expected for much of this week. The upper level 
trough, which moved over the area today, will sit to our east for
the next couple of days. Another trough will develop over the 
West Coast by early Wednesday. In between these two systems, we'll
have weak ridging aloft over/just west of the area. Temperatures 
will gradually warm through the work week, with highs reaching 
near 60 for much of the plains by Friday. Thanksgiving looks
rather pleasant weather-wise, with highs in the 50s across the
plains and partly cloudy skies. Mid and upper level northwest 
winds will strengthen on Friday, bringing moisture into the area.
Models vary with the depth of the upper level shortwave and the 
placement of the upper level jet. Some runs have a broader 
longwave trough with the jet more to our north, putting us in the 
right exit region. While other runs, have a more potent shortwave
and put us in the left exit region of the jet. The latter would 
be much more favorable for precip and cooler temperatures in our 
area, with the most likely timing of precip sometime late Friday 
through early Sunday. Looking at cluster analysis, about 55% of 
the ensemble members in the LREF bring a deeper shortwave with 
light rain making it off the higher terrain and into the plains on
Saturday, with 19% of the ensemble members bringing snow onto the
plains.

Temperatures and precip chances will remain highly uncertain for the 
weekend and into Monday. 25th to 75th percentile highs differ by 
around 10 to 15 degrees across the plains during this time.
Precipitation chances will depend heavily on what happens with 
this first system mentioned above and a potential second shortwave
Monday.

.AVIATION /06Z TAFS through 06Z Wednesday/...
Issued at 1033 PM MST Mon Nov 23 2026

With low stratus deck in place at all terminals, MVFR conditions 
will prevail tonight. Current observations have yet to show fog 
developing over the plains, so confidence is increasing that fog 
will not impact the airports by the early morning hours. However, 
there is still a 10-15% chance of fog with 1/4SM visibility, 
particularly between 09Z-14Z.

VFR conditions are expected to return by 11Z-14Z when winds 
transition to a more southwesterly component and push the low 
ceilings out. For today, generally light (<10 kts) winds will 
prevail with east/southeast winds in the afternoon transitioning to 
a light drainage overnight.
