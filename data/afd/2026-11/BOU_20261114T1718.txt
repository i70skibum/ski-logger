.UPDATE...
Issued at 501 PM MST Sat Nov 14 2026

Fog has begun to develop over parts of the east Colorado plains. 
Current webcams and observations show visibilities deteriorating 
over much of Lincoln County and parts of Elbert County, particularly 
along I-70. We have opted to issue a Dense Fog Advisory for Lincoln 
County at this time, but it may need to be expanded to surrounding 
counties as the fog continues to spread in the overnight hours. 
Visibilities may drop to less than 1/4 mile at times, and this has 
already been observed in Limon over the last hour. We expect 
visibilities to improve by late Sunday morning. We have adjusted 
low temperatures over the eastern Colorado plains tonight to 
better reflect the heat-trapping effect of the fog and low clouds.

.DISCUSSION /Through Saturday/...
Issued at 1239 PM MST Sat Nov 14 2026

A negatively tilted trough is moving over the area this afternoon. 
Moisture has increased significantly at mid and upper levels as this 
system approached the area. As of early this afternoon, the DCVZ 
was just to the east of Denver, with very light showers to its 
east and north. We still have a bit of a dry layer around 750mb 
across the plains, and with how light the precip is, it could 
struggle to make it to the ground in some areas until the layer 
starts to saturate more. Overall rain amounts are expected to be 
very light for much of the plains. Snow showers are moving into 
the mountains from the west and south as of early this afternoon. 
Coverage of snow showers this afternoon and evening will be best 
south of I-70, with most likely accumulations around a trace to 3 
inches above 9000ft. Elevations around 8000ft to 9000ft could see 
a rain/snow mix today, but accumulations are unlikely. 

Overnight, we're expecting more fog development across the plains. 
The REFS currently has a medium to high chance (50-75%) that 
portions of the I-70 and northern I-25 corridors will see fog with
visiblities less than 0.5 mile in the early morning. Fog should 
start to clear around 10AM give or take an hour. 

Upper level ridging begins to build over the area again on 
Sunday. Warmer and drier conditions will prevail Sunday through 
much of Tuesday. On Tuesday, an upper level trough will move onto
the West Coast. Winds at mid levels will strengthen as the 
pressure gradient tightens between the upper ridge and trough. 
However, the strongest winds are forecast to remain to the south 
and east of our area. The trough will weaken and move east, 
sending a shortwave through the area early Wednesday. Precip 
chances will increase late Tuesday in the higher terrain as the 
shortwave approaches. The shortwave will be moving rather quickly 
and the southwesterly to westerly winds aloft are not super 
favorable for our area, so impacts from this event will be rather 
limited with snow totals around an inch or less for the higher 
elevations. For the plains, a cold front is forecast to move 
through sometime Wednesday, bringing slightly cooler temps for 
Wednesday into the end of the work week. Rain chances remain low 
(<20%) for the plains with the this system.

That shortwave will quickly move off to the northeast as an upper 
low moves onto the West Coast. The progression of this upper 
low/trough Thursday into the weekend is highly uncertain. A 
couple of days ago, models had a slower moving closed low headed 
towards the four corners region, now the most recent runs have a 
quicker moving more open wave going over and out of the area by 
late Saturday. The former is much more favorable for snowfall 
across our area, which is why we've seen a steady downward trend 
in precip amounts in the ensemble members. However, we are still 
quite a ways out, so things could swing back the other direction.
We'll be keeping a close eye on this system.

.AVIATION /00Z TAFS through 00Z Monday/...
Issued at 508 PM MST Sat Nov 14 2026

A boundary is currently moving over the airport at KDEN which is 
shifting winds from the southeast to the northwest or west. The 
west/northwest winds will continue at all terminals this evening. 

The main concern tonight will be fog/low clouds which will reduce
visibility. Fog at BJC and DEN is possible anywhere from 04Z
through 18Z Sunday. However, the most likely timing for fog at BJC
and DEN is between 09-10Z and 16Z. When the fog is in place,
visibility should be between 1/4 and 1/2 SM. There may be breaks
from time to time in the fog at both airports throughout the early
morning hours. With a surface cyclone well to the north of Denver,
the west winds may push the fog away from the airports at times.
At APA, fog is certainly possible, but unlikely (30% chance). The
fog that develops should be very close to to APA but should stay
just to the north. 

After the fog mixes out, VFR conditions with east winds are
expected for the rest of the day. Some gusts in the late afternoon
could reach 18 knots at DEN. Winds will turn southeasterly then to
drainage during the evening and overnight.
